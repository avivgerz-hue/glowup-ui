# Independent visual review

Use `blind-review.mjs` as described in [PAIRED-EVALUATION.md](PAIRED-EVALUATION.md). Prepare comparable desktop/mobile captures with identical data, browser and dimensions. It randomizes consistent X/Y labels and puts the key outside the reviewer directory.

Give reviewers only the images/reviewer form. Freeze their scores and reasons before revealing the key. Assess hierarchy, layout, typography, product fit, consistency, mobile behavior and polish; functional/accessibility checks remain separate. Preserve losses and ties. Generated forms contain no aesthetic scores, and label blinding cannot guarantee that distinctive visual styles are unrecognizable.
