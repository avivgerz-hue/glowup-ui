# Share an installation result or real example

[Open an optional experience report](https://github.com/avivgerz-hue/glowup-ui/issues/new?template=experience.yml), or [report a reproducible bug](https://github.com/avivgerz-hue/glowup-ui/issues/new?template=bug.yml).

Reports are **public**, and GitHub displays your account. Text alone is enough: version/commit, agent version, OS, install method, what you verified, and what failed. Never post credentials, personal email addresses, local paths, private application/client details or raw transcripts. Use fictional data in images and share only material you have permission to publish. Sensitive findings follow [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md); no private reporting channel or anonymity is promised by this form.

Distinguish installed files, discovery, full instruction loading and actual implementation. An installation success is not evidence of better design. If sharing a real example, state CREATE/TRANSFORM/REFINE, authorship (agent/manual/scripted), functional checks and limitations. Before/after images should use matching data and viewport sizes. We do not require source disclosure from private apps.

Glowup adds no analytics, tracking pixel or background submission. Feedback is voluntary and will not be republished as a testimonial without separate permission. Maintainers must label self-reports as self-reports, keep failures visible, and not convert them into unverified adoption totals or quality scores. Third-party installers and GitHub have their own privacy practices; the documented standard-install command disables its telemetry.
